About the Cyber Tech Prototype: Capabilities & Operational Significance

Engineered for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) under the Blockchain & Cybersecurity theme, Cyber Tech delivers autonomous, passive cryptographic posture auditing for mission-critical email backbones without requiring server private keys or interrupting active message routing.

What the Prototype Does

Cyber Tech is an end-to-end passive network forensics system designed to evaluate the cryptographic security posture of SMTP, IMAP, and POP3 communications:

  • Zero-Touch Stream Reassembly: Pure-Python PCAP/PCAPNG packet parser and TCP stream engine that reconstructs bidirectional sessions without third-party C-bindings.
  • STARTTLS Stripping Downgrade Interception: Tracks protocol state transitions to catch active Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) actors stripping `250-STARTTLS` announcements and sniffing cleartext credentials.
  • TLS Handshake Dissection & JA3-512: Dissects ClientHello/ServerHello records, evaluates 350+ IANA cipher suites, verifies Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS), and computes 512-bit SHA-512 JA3 hashes.
  • Pure ASN.1 DER X.509 Certificate Engine: Validates certificate expiration, public key bit lengths (RSA/ECC), weak hashing algorithms (SHA-1/MD5), and self-signed status.
  • Multi-Factor Posture Scoring (0–100): Combines protocol integrity, cipher robustness, PFS, certificate hygiene, and anomaly penalties into standard grades (A+ to F).
  • Dual AI/ML Architecture: Houses a 5-class supervised ensemble risk classifier alongside an unsupervised Isolation Forest model for zero-day TLS anomaly detection.
  • Automated NLP Remediation: Generates executive summaries, MITRE ATT&CK mappings, and ready-to-deploy Postfix and Dovecot hardening configurations.

Significance & Strategic Impact

Email remains the single most targeted communication vector for cyber espionage, state-sponsored interception, and business email compromise (BEC):

  • Eliminates the "Silent Downgrade" Blindspot: Traditional mail transfer agents (MTAs) silently fall back to plaintext when opportunistic TLS fails, leaving organizations completely unaware of ongoing eavesdropping.
  • Zero Disruption & Privacy Preserving: Does not require server SSL/TLS private keys and does not decrypt confidential email message bodies, fully preserving end-user privacy while analyzing cryptographic metadata.
  • Critical Infrastructure & Defense Protection: Empowers national security agencies, government email gateways (e.g. NIC, defense ministries), and banking networks to enforce mandatory encryption baselines.
  • Section 65B Forensic Admissibility: By anchoring every audit finding into a 512-bit SHA-512 Merkle blockchain ledger, Cyber Tech produces mathematically unforgeable chain-of-custody evidence admissible in courts of law.
  • Bridges Packet Decoders to Immediate Remediation: Unlike Wireshark or tcpdump which merely display raw hex packets, Cyber Tech translates cryptographic defects into copy-paste configuration directives for SOC and SysAdmin teams.

Technical Innovation Pillars

How Cyber Tech achieves unparalleled architectural efficiency:

  • Zero Mandatory External C-Libraries: Complete network engine runs entirely on Python's standard library, ensuring immediate, fail-safe cloud deployment across Linux, macOS, and Render containers.
  • NIST FIPS 180-4 512-Bit SHA-512 Ledger: Elevates traditional 256-bit hash chains to collision-resistant 512-bit Merkle structures for quantum-resilient forensic archiving.
  • Dual-Mode Runtime: Operates seamlessly as an interactive Security Operations Center (SOC) web application, an automated headless CLI tool, or a cloud REST API.
